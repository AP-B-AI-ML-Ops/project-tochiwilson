# Renewable Energy Forecasting (Antwerp Region)

## Problem Description
The renewable energy transition creates grid instability because solar and wind production are weather-dependent. Grid operators and energy traders need reliable short-term forecasts to make balancing decisions. 

In this project, we build an end-to-end Machine Learning system that predicts the renewable energy production.

* **What is predicted:** The solar and/or wind energy production in Megawatts (MW) for the Antwerp region over the next 24 hours.
* **Model Inputs:** Hourly weather forecast data (Wind speed in km/h and/or Solar radiation in W/m²) from the Open-Meteo ECMWF model.
* **Model Output:** Predicted energy production (MW) per hour for the next 24 hours.
* **Why this is useful:** Accurate forecasts allow grid operators to balance the power grid, prevent blackouts, and allow energy traders to optimize their buying and selling strategies on the Day-Ahead market.
